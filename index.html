How iFocus measures focus

Focus score (0–100)

A blended estimate of how focused you are right now, combining your blink rate, head stillness, and gaze stability. Higher = more focused.

Blinks per minute BPM

Healthy reading averages ~15 blinks/min. Much lower can mean strain; much higher can mean fatigue (Andreassi, 2000).

Head movement

How much your head shifts second-to-second. Frequent head movement often correlates with distraction.

Gaze direction

Where you're looking on the screen. The gaze map (during a session) shows your live attention point; the heatmap (in the report) shows where you spent the most time.

Calibrate personal model

A short typing + arithmetic exercise that learns your personal "focused" baseline. Optional, but recommended for accuracy.

Nudge with music

If you stay distracted past your chosen threshold, a gentle tone plays to bring you back. Tap the indicator to silence.

Privacy

Everything runs on your device. No video, gaze data, or session history ever leaves your computer.
